What the JabCon board collects, and about whom

The JabCon board is a wall display for JabCon, the JabRef developer meetup. It shows what the participants are working on while the meetup runs. Because it names people, the GDPR applies, and this page is the notice required by its Articles 13 and 14.

Who is responsible

The JabCon organisers, for the JabRef project. Reach them by opening an issue in JabRef/jabcon-board or by writing to privacy@jabref.org.

What is shown, and where it comes from

Public GitHub activity of the participants
Login, pull requests and issues with their titles and links, reviews, comment excerpts, pushes and the time each of these happened — read from the public GitHub search and events APIs, in any public repository.
Posts to the configured mailing lists
Sender, subject and an excerpt, read from the public archive. The public name and e-mail address of a participant's GitHub profile are used to recognise their posts; neither is published or stored.
Private repositories
Only counts per participant, never titles, numbers or texts.

None of this is collected from the participants themselves; all of it is activity they published on GitHub or on a public mailing list. Nothing is collected about you for looking at this page: the board is a static site, sets no cookies, runs no analytics and loads nothing from anybody else. Its hosting is GitHub Pages, whose own logging is described in GitHub's privacy statement.

Why

To make the meetup's shared work visible in the room while it happens, and to keep the friendly competition on the leaderboard going. The legal basis is the participants' consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), which they gave when they joined the board; without it, our legitimate interest in showing the meetup's own work (Art. 6(1)(f)).

The awards rank what somebody contributed. They deliberately do not rank when a person works, or whether they used an AI assistant: the board does not draw a picture of a person's habits.

How long

The board has been discontinued for privacy reasons. The published data and all screenshots of the board have been deleted; nothing is collected any more.

Your rights

You can ask for access, rectification, erasure, restriction and data portability, object to the processing (Art. 21), withdraw your consent at any time with effect for the future, and complain to a supervisory authority. In practice the quickest route is to ask us to take you off the board: your login is removed from the configuration and your cards, activity and points are gone from the next update, a few minutes later.
